What is TSA?

The Technology Student Association is a national organization for students passionate about STEM. Through competitive events and chapter activities, members develop technical skills, leadership, and teamwork.

Our chapter competes at regional, state, and national levels across dozens of events — from coding and engineering to public speaking and video production.
